# Privacy Policy for ZenoPay Wallet

Last updated: December 2025

ZenoPay Wallet ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the ZenoPay Wallet mobile application.

## Information We Collect
We may collect the following information:
- Personal information (name, email address, phone number)
- Identity verification data (ID documents and selfies for KYC)
- Wallet and transaction data
- Device and usage data

## How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Verify your identity (KYC)
- Process wallet transactions
- Improve app security and performance
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only with:
- Identity verification providers
- Payment and wallet service providers
- Legal authorities when required by law

## Data Security
We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption and access controls.

## Data Retention
Your data is retained only as long as necessary to provide services or comply with legal obligations.

## Account Deletion
You can permanently delete your account from within the app:
Settings → Security → Delete Account  
This will delete your profile, wallet, KYC data, and transaction history.

## Your Rights
You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data.
