Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZenoPay collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide app features, protect accounts, process wallet activity, and improve service quality.

  • Account information such as your full name, email address, phone number, city, country, date of birth, and other details you provide when creating or updating your account.
  • Profile information such as your avatar, profile photo, and any information you choose to add inside the app.
  • Wallet and transaction information such as deposits, withdrawals, transfers, balances, and transaction history.
  • Order and purchase information related to products or services available in the app, including mobile shop orders, gift cards, top-up cards, and virtual card creation.
  • Account protection information such as ID images, selfie images, or other verification details when required for security, fraud prevention, or account review.
  • Support and communication information when you contact us, request help, reset your password, or use account recovery features.
  • Location information only when you allow it, for example to help suggest your city in supported pages of the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only for purposes related to operating, securing, maintaining, and improving the app.

  • To create, manage, and protect your account.
  • To provide wallet features and process account activity.
  • To process purchases, orders, and related services.
  • To display account details, balances, and transaction history inside the app.
  • To protect users, review suspicious activity, and reduce fraud or misuse.
  • To support login verification, password reset, and account recovery.
  • To improve app performance, reliability, security, and user experience.
  • To respond to support requests and send important service-related updates.

3. Location Information

If you allow location access, ZenoPay may use it only to help suggest or display your city in supported parts of the app. You may still manually enter your city where available.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by secure service providers or internal admin tools only when needed to operate the app, support users, maintain security, store account data, or process orders and wallet activity.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your information and reduce unauthorized access, misuse, or loss. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to operate the app, maintain records, protect accounts, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and improve service reliability.

7. Your Choices

You may review or update certain account details inside the app. You may also contact support regarding account information, privacy-related requests, or security concerns.

8. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on trusted external services such as hosting, authentication, storage, notifications, or infrastructure providers. These services may process information only as needed to support app functionality.

9. Children

ZenoPay is intended for users who are at least 18 years old.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updated, the latest version will appear in the app or on the official page with a revised last updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us.
